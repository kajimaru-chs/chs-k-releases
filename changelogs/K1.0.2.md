# Custom Hero Survival K1.0.2 Z2.6 M9.18

Fix special Summon Tech purchases that consumed lumber without applying research
and could be bought indefinitely.

- Restore native research for Evasion, Slow Poison, Hardened Skin, Elune's Grace, Critical Strike, and Magic Attack.
- Each upgrade costs 10 lumber and stops at level 10. Purchases at the cap refund the lumber and show the original 18k maximum-level message.
- Apply the original native summon ability bindings, including the existing unit-specific exceptions. Judgement's Crusader keeps its original critical/evasion exceptions.
- Preserve current shop availability, Summon Attack/Defense Tech, and Mage Tech.
- Refresh the lobby description with creator credits, a new map summary, and the themed download website. Tripled `|n` separators space the sections.
- Launch the K Edition download website with direct normal-map downloads and full changelogs. The website lists official releases only.

The preview, loading layout, terrain, and other gameplay are unchanged. This is
not a fix for the unresolved immediate online-disconnection reports.
