# K1.9

- Added personal flying couriers for shopping, storage, crafting and delivery between rounds. Use F2 to select, B to Blink and D to deliver.
- Shops follow your selected hero or courier. Sell All affects only the selected inventory, and purchases can complete recipes even with a full inventory.
- Arena Champion is now a permanent buff with the Hope aura, freeing an inventory slot. Its healing triggers once per second and respects Kaji and Xyn Kaif.
- Reduced Judgement's Crusader summon cooldown from 30 to 20 seconds.
- Removed Wand of Mana's active ability; its passive bonuses remain unchanged.
