# K1.1 RC2

Release candidate for testing. K1.0.3 remains the current official version.

- Increase Morvath's visual size by 30%.
- Reduce his base attack interval from 2.0 to 1.5 seconds.
- Reduce his cast point from 1.2 to 0.8 seconds.
- Give Arcane Convergence's three spheres distinct colors: yellow, aqua and lilac.
- Shorten Morvath's hero information and match the other heroes' title, attribute and passive formatting.

Arcane Convergence's damage, charge cycle and Pyroblast behavior are unchanged from RC1.
