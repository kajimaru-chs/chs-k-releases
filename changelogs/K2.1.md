# K2.1

## Heroes

- **Zaron** — Liutenant
  - Gains **Returning Blades**: his next three attacks ricochet for up to 20 hits per blade within 600 range, prioritizing enemies hit fewer times.
  - Each enemy can be hit three times per blade, taking 100%, 80% and 50% weapon damage. Blades can return through Zaron when needed to reach another eligible enemy.
  - Unique attack modifiers are suspended for these three attacks and restored afterward. Ordinary attacks keep their original bouncing behavior.
  - Active ricochets use his normal spinning weapon and continue correctly after a target dies.
- **Unbroken**
  - Gains **Scarlet Roar**: he and his living illusions gain 100% bonus attack speed and can pass through units for 5 seconds. Cooldown: 30 seconds.
  - Enraged illusions have a 12% chance on hit to create an ordinary Mirage. Unbroken's own passive chance remains unchanged.
  - Existing passive Mirages regain their full lifetime when buffed.
- **Zenji**
  - Gains **Ancestral Renewal**, doubling health regeneration for 10 seconds. Cooldown: 90 seconds.
  - Includes regeneration from attributes, items, auras and his passive; excludes lifesteal and instant healing.
- **Snikch**
  - Missile Barrage can target anywhere within his current arena without approaching the target. The entire impact circle stays inside the arena boundaries.
  - Barrages cast during the arena countdown continue when the wave appears.
- **Vlad**
  - Returns to his normal form when travelling between shops and combat arenas, including PvP and Battle Royale.
  - True Form retains its 20-second duration and 90-second cooldown.
- **Judgement**
  - Calls out yellow floating battle cries as his soldier reaches evolution milestones. His champion's final call repeats from evolution level 200 onward.

## Combat and presentation

- Effects spanning the arena countdown now retain their original timing when the wave begins, including Astaroth, Medusa, Returning Blades, Summoner's Orb and Mavina's burn and explosion effects.
- Added status-bar indicators and distinctive visuals for Returning Blades, Scarlet Roar and Ancestral Renewal.
