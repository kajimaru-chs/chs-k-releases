# Custom Hero Survival K1.0 Z2.6 M9.18

Preserved K Edition development milestone, released on November 3, 2026.

- Modular implementation with the original 18k map as the behavior reference.
- Creep Rage supports Isolated, Shared, and Disabled. Disabled removes Greed purchases.
- Caff stores up to 15 charges. Unused charges cause 0.5 seconds of silence each on expiration; pending effects are cleared at wave end.
- Reusable K Edition lobby preview. The original gameplay minimap is restored once during initialization, with no recurring preview timer.
- Original 18k terrain and the approved loading layout and credits are preserved.
- Summon Tech and Mage Tech retain their current initial availability.
