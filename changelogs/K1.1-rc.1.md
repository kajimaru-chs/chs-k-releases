# K1.1 RC1

Release candidate for testing. K1.0.3 remains the current official version.

## Morvath — Arcane Monolith

- Replaces Marley, using Kael's native model and portrait.
- Gains 2 Strength, 1 Agility and 3 Intelligence per level. Starts with 20 Strength, 10 Agility and 20 Intelligence, with 100 base mana before attribute bonuses.
- Has the slowest base casting animation in the roster: a 1.2-second cast point and 1.6-second backswing.
- New passive, **Arcane Convergence**: the first three active skill casts create three orbiting spheres; the fourth consumes them and empowers that cast's spell damage. Repeated skills count.
- Grants 20% additional spell damage through level 15, scaling linearly to 200% at level 100. This adds to Mage Tech: +400% from Mage Tech and +200% from Convergence total +600%, or seven times the base damage.
- Buffs, healing and summoning skills also charge and consume the spheres, but their healing, bonuses and summoned units are not amplified. Item abilities and passive effects do not charge the spheres.
- Pyroblast is included. Death and Decay counts toward the cycle but is never amplified. Charges clear on death and at the end of combat.
- Removes Marley's former curse passive and its exclusive K'Ajim Aru interaction.

## Pyroblast

- No target is required to begin casting. Preparation takes 9 seconds at level 1, decreasing to 7 seconds at level 20.
- Fires once after preparation, then four more times at 10-second intervals. Each launch selects the nearest visible, valid enemy within the skill's range from the caster's current position.
- A launch with no valid target is lost. It does not wait for an enemy or refund the cooldown.
- Preserves missile damage, mana cost, range and cooldown. The initial missile stuns for 4 seconds; subsequent missiles stun for 1 second.
