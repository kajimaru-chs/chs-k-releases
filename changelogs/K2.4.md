# K2.4

## Heroes

- **Huron**
  - Ravaging Cyclone no longer ends when hit by Volcano or silenced, while preserving its normal model and moving spin.
  - Spellbreaker no longer drains mana while Ravaging Cyclone is active.
  - Increased Ravaging Cyclone cooldown from 60 to 90 seconds.
  - Slow Aura continues to slow Huron during Ravaging Cyclone.
