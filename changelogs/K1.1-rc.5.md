# K1.1 RC5 — Morvath visual adjustments

Testing candidate. K1.0.3 remains the current official release.

- Reduce Morvath's size by 20% compared with RC4.
- Move his hero information button to the top-left skill slot.
- Give Arcane Convergence red, aqua and yellow spheres, in that order.
- Use separate native-derived orb effects for Classic, Reforged/HD and Definitive Edition, preserving each mode's effect style.

The passive still follows its existing three-charge/fourth-cast damage cycle. Random elements and combination spells are not implemented. Orb colors, mode-specific rendering and health/mana bar clearance need in-game confirmation; this candidate does not claim an independent bar-height fix.
