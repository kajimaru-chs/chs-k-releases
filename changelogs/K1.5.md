# K1.5

- Removed the Treant. 👋
- Introduced **Rhazek, the Bloodreaper**. 🩸
- Increased Valdran's counterattack chance to 25% and added an independent spectral strike against nearby melee attackers.
- Enemy hero kills now reset Death Knight's survival recovery.
- Naga can move and cast during Pyroblast preparation, Flame Strike and Devastating Drain. Her passive Frost Breath supports Enchanted Diamond and Caff, but does not trigger Codex of Caine.
- Caff gains charges from more offensive spells, including Codex of Caine and Enchanted Diamond procs. Its release can target a nearby enemy and bypasses Runeforged Plates' spell block.
- Increased Enchanted Diamond's automatic targeting range.
- Flame Strike and Devastating Drain now tick every 0.1 seconds, preserving base damage rates.
- May now wears a white hood.
- Effects cast during the arena countdown continue when the wave begins.
- Removed unused legacy resources.

Starfall and Death & Decay still stop when Naga moves
