# K1.1

A new hero and Pyroblast improvements.

## Heroes

- 👋 Removed Marley, the Witch Doctor.
- Introducing Morvath, the Elemental Sage.

## Pyroblast

- No target required: each missile seeks the nearest enemy in range.
- Preparation scales from 9 to 7 seconds. Fires one missile, then four more at 10-second intervals.
- Added preparation animations, orange cast text and a consistent shop/ability icon.

## Other changes

- Added floating feedback when Siren or Morvath attempts to buy Whisper of the Waning Moon.
