# K2.1.1

## Heroes

- **Mau'yo**
  - Adds a Classic Tauren Chieftain model and portrait fallback.
  - Evolution of Forms grants +100% movement speed through a distinct self-only aura instead of the former STR-based flat bonus. Other evolution bonuses are unchanged.
- **Snikch**
  - Repairs a missing Classic missile-smoke texture.
  - Restores the K2.0 description. Global casting within the current arena remains unchanged.

## Items

- **Divine Source**
  - Restores its cleanse effect with a valid native model in Classic, HD and Definitive.
- **Summoner's Orb**
  - Clarifies that each valid summon basic hit instantly heals its owner for 0.15% maximum HP at any distance. Healing behavior and existing suppression rules are unchanged.
