# K1.4.1

Refined Morvath, Chainlight Storm and K'Ajim Aru.

- Reduced Morvath's attack range.
- Pulverize and Corrosive Skin can now apply Chainlight Storm marks.
- Added Intelligence scaling to K'Ajim Aru's burn and expanded its healing block to negative effects.
- Envenomed Weapons now ticks every 0.5 seconds, preserving base DPS.
