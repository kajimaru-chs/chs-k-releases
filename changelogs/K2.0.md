# K2.0

New hero abilities, evolving summons and item updates.

## Heroes

- **Mavina**
  - Her companion evolves into a Black Dragon, Red Dragon and Frost Wyrm.
  - Evolutions increase range and add explosive attacks or slowing splash attacks.
- **Vlad**
  - Gains True Form, temporarily increasing his base attributes and movement speed.
- **Huron**
  - Gains Ravaging Cyclone, dealing physical damage while spinning and moving with spell immunity.
  - Spin duration and attack frequency improve with hero level.
- **Diana**
  - Gains Veil of a Thousand Blades, becoming invisible in dark smoke and striking nearby enemies with attribute-based magic damage.
- **Artemis**
  - Gains Venom Frenzy, temporarily increasing poison chance and attack speed.
- **Bernstein**
  - Gains Devour, earning permanent health from consumed enemies and dealing true damage to heroes.
  - Rot damage recalibrated.
- **Sasha**
  - Gains Nature's Reproach, causing affected enemies to miss attacks against her, with the chance scaling with agility.
- **Naga Siren**
  - Gains Tidal Blink, traveling as a wave and dealing intelligence-based damage along its path.
  - Travel distance increases with hero level.
- **Dhar'leth**
  - Discharges grant permanent maximum mana.
  - Gains Arcane Fire Armor, converting maximum mana into temporary armor.
- **Berserker**
  - Gains Ragerush, jumping to an enemy and accelerating his next attacks.
- **Snikch**
  - Gains Missile Barrage, launching more missiles as he levels up.
  - Impacts deal magic damage, reduce armor and reveal invisible enemies.
- **Regal Gorgon**
  - Gains Essence Theft, giving basic attacks a chance to steal temporary agility from each enemy.
  - The chance increases with hero level, and the effect works with Multishot.
- **Mau'yo**
  - Evolved summons gain movement speed based on his strength and can pass through other units.
- **Rhazek**
  - Increased healing reduction.
- **May**
  - Guardian Angel can be activated while stunned.

## Items

- **Summoner's Orb**
  - Adds summon damage based on the owner's primary attribute and heals the owner when summons hit.
  - Its active adds damage based on a percentage of the target's current health.
  - Locust Swarm and Mavina's dragon are excluded from these additional effects.
- **Bow of the Creators**
  - Increases attack range. Melee heroes and Rifleman cannot purchase it.
- **Caff's Scepter**
  - Deals exact true damage, ignoring damage reductions and shields.
  - Increased armor penalty.
- **K'Ajim Aru**
  - Its burn deals exact true damage, ignoring damage reductions and shields.
  - Reduced maximum-health penalty and increased maximum-mana bonus.
- **Divine Source**
  - Can be activated while disabled to cleanse negative effects.
