# K1.3

- Removed Charles & Shaggy, the Mortar Team. 👋
- Introduced **Glacius, the Ice Ghost**. ❄️
- Glacius brings a seven-second freezing storm with microstuns, chill and Mage Tech scaling against heroes. Basic attacks chill enemies; the former attack and death explosions are removed.
- Added a custom Glacius portrait and denser ice-storm visuals. His channel supports stationary invisibility and continues through wave start.
- Morvath: primary basic hits now have a 20% chance to grant a random sphere and trigger the combination when three spheres are ready. Illusions and Multishot secondary hits are excluded.
- Morvath: silence clears spheres and prevents generating or triggering combinations. Attack interval reduced from 1.70 to 1.60 seconds.
- Lord Archi: level-up growth adjusted to +3.5 STR and +3 INT. Lightning marks cannot be applied or refreshed on invisible, invulnerable or untargetable enemies.
- Increased spacing between Sell All and the gold/lumber buttons.
